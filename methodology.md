# Derank Index Methodology (2026-11)

> The scoring method is public, verifiable, challengeable and overturnable.

## Formula

Derank Index = Public Score x60% + Editorial Score x40%; Editorial = Dao 25% + Fa 30% + Li 15% + Yu 15% + Shou 15%.

## Iron rules

- Every issue must contain at least one downgrade and one upgrade, with reasons.
- No paid placements, no paid reviews.
- Errors are corrected publicly, never silently.

## Data cutoff

2026-11. Scores in the ranking were compiled from Derank's 4 verified category datasets; each cites public sources. ratingCount=1 reflects a single editorial score, not a crowd sample.
